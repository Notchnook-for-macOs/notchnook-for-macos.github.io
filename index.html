NotchNook

Transform the unused space around your MacBook notch into a functional, interactive workspace. NotchNook is a lightweight, highly customizable utility that adds smart system indicators, ambient widgets, performance tools, and aesthetic personalization—without impacting macOS performance.

Customizable Status Bar Extensions

Extend your status bar with tailored indicators for system stats, notifications, and quick controls. Position and style them precisely around the notch for at-a-glance clarity.

Ambient Backgrounds & Visual Effects

Apply subtle, dynamic backgrounds and effects that enhance your desktop without distraction. Choose from smooth gradients, live animations, or custom themes that adapt to your workflow.

Widget Dock Around the Notch

Dock essential widgets—clock, calendar, notes, and more—directly in the notch area. Access them instantly with intuitive gestures, keeping your screen space pristine.

Performance-Conscious Design

Engineered for efficiency, NotchNook runs silently in the background. No lag, no drain—powered by native macOS APIs for seamless integration.

Flexible Appearance Settings
